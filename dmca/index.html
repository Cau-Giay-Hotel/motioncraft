  5. A statement, made under penalty of perjury, that the information in your notice is accurate and that you are the copyright owner or authorized to act on the owner's behalf;

We review each complete notice. For a style template, we can remove or replace it directly. For a generated video result stored on our AI processing provider's infrastructure, we will forward your request to that provider and ask them to remove it — please see our Privacy Policy §9 for why we cannot currently guarantee automatic or immediate removal of content already on our provider's server, and for what we can remove ourselves. We will review counter-notices and, where appropriate and where it is within our control, may restore access.

We aim to acknowledge copyright complaints and counter-notices within a reasonable time.
