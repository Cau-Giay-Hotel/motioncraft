FAQ
Is MotionCraft really free?

Yes. Every template, every result count and every profile tab is open from the first launch — there is no subscription and no in-app purchase in this version.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-in screen at all. Your likes, follows and creation history are kept on your device, and you can delete them any time from Settings.

What happens to the photos I upload?

They're used only to generate the result you asked for, and are automatically removed if unused. You can delete them, and all your other local data, any time from Settings.

What languages does it support?

14 interface languages, including Hindi, Bahasa Indonesia, Thai, Vietnamese, Bengali, Urdu and Arabic — with right-to-left layout for Urdu and Arabic.

Can I report something I see in the community feed?

Yes. Every comment, post and creator profile has a Report or Block option, and reported content is hidden from your view right away while our team reviews it.
