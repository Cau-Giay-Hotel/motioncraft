FAQ
Is MotionCraft really free?

Yes. Every template and every generation is free in this version — there is no subscription and no in-app purchase.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-in screen at all. Your creation history is kept on your device, and you can delete it any time from Settings.

Why does it take about a minute and a half?

Each video is generated fresh by an AI model — it's a real render, not a quick filter. You can leave the app; we'll notify you when it's ready.

What happens to the photo I upload?

It's sent to our server and processed by a third-party AI video service to generate your video. You can delete your local creation history and other on-device data any time from Settings; that doesn't remove a photo or video already on our provider's server, but you can email us to request removal of a specific one (a manual, best-effort process).

What languages does it support?

14 interface languages, including Hindi, Bahasa Indonesia, Thai, Vietnamese, Bengali, Urdu and Arabic — with right-to-left layout for Urdu and Arabic.
