1. No account, no sign-in

MotionCraft does not have user accounts, registration, or login (no email/password, no Google/Facebook sign-in). You can use every feature of the App immediately after install.

Instead of an account, the App generates a random device identifier (a UUID) the first time you open it, and stores it only on your device. This identifier is sent with each request to our AI processing provider only so their support team can look up a specific request in their logs (for example, to troubleshoot a failed generation) — it is not used to build a profile of you, and it is not linked to your name, email, phone number, or any other real-world identity, because we never ask for one. The App itself authenticates to that provider with a key that belongs to the App, not to you.

You can erase this identifier and the data associated with it that lives on your device at any time: Settings ▸ Delete my data. See §9 ("Your rights") for exactly what this does and does not remove.

2. Photos you use in the App — uploaded to our AI processing provider

MotionCraft lets you pick one photo from your device (using the Android system Photo Picker) and choose a style template. The App then generates a short AI video from that photo.

  • We do not request access to your photo library. The system Photo Picker lets you hand us the specific photo you choose without granting the App any broader storage permission.
  • Before your photo leaves your device, the App rotates it to the correct orientation and then strips all EXIF metadata, including GPS/location coordinates, resizes it, and compresses it. Your photo's location data never reaches our servers or our AI processing provider.
  • Your prepared photo is uploaded to a third-party server to generate the result you asked for. MotionCraft is a client for the media-forge platform (operated by our technology partner, Stemlabs), which receives your photo over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection and passes it to a third-party AI model — currently MiniMax's Hailuo-02 image-to-video model, run through the AI infrastructure provider fal.ai — to produce your video. This is a change from earlier versions of the App, which processed a built-in sample dataset only on your device: the current version of the App sends your chosen photo off your device to be processed by these third parties.
  • We do not use your photos to train our own AI models. We do not control, and cannot make representations about, whether our AI processing provider or its own model provider use submitted images to improve their systems; if you want details of their own data-handling practices, contact us and we will pass your question along, or avoid uploading photos you are not comfortable sharing with a third-party AI service.

3. Your video results are stored at a public link (not protected by a login)

The video MotionCraft generates for you is stored as an .mp4 file on our AI provider's content delivery network (CDN), at a URL that looks like https://media-forge.b-cdn.net/apps/ai-video-india/jobs/<job id>/output.mp4.

This URL is not protected by any password, login, or access check. The address itself is long and effectively impossible to guess, and we do not publish, list, or link it anywhere public — but anyone who obtains the exact link (for example, if you share it, or if it is exposed by your device, a messaging app, or a network in between) will be able to view that video without needing an account. Please keep this in mind before you share a result link outside the App, and avoid generating a video from a photo you would not want viewable by anyone who happens to get hold of that link.

4. Likes and saved styles stay on your device

When you like a style or save it for later, that choice is stored only in local app storage on your device (Android DataStore). It is not sent to our server, not shared with anyone, and is lost if you delete the App's data or uninstall the App.

5. Feedback

If you use Settings ▸ Send feedback, the App opens your device's own email app with our support address pre-filled. We do not collect anything through this feature until you choose to send that email — the message content, and anything you attach, is transmitted the same way any email you send is transmitted (by your email provider), not by us.

7. Third-party services

MotionCraft uses the following third-party services:

  • media-forge (operated by Stemlabs) — receives the photo you submit and returns your generated video. This is the service described in §2 and §3.
  • fal.ai, running MiniMax's Hailuo-02 image-to-video model — the AI infrastructure and model that actually turns your photo into a video, on behalf of media-forge. We do not have a direct relationship with fal.ai or MiniMax; media-forge is our contracted processor for this feature.
  • Firebase Analytics (Google) — app usage analytics.
  • Firebase Crashlytics (Google) — crash and stability reporting.
  • Firebase Remote Config (Google) — lets us adjust app behavior/configuration without a new release.
  • Google Mobile Ads / AdMob — the App's build includes Google's advertising SDK and a Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent component. In the current version of the App, ads are not served — this infrastructure exists so a future version can show ads, and where required by your region's privacy law (e.g., GDPR/UK, or a Google-defined US state), the App will present a consent/privacy choices prompt before any ad-related data is used. If and when ads are turned on in a later version, this Policy will be updated to describe the ad formats shown.
  • Adjust — mobile attribution/analytics (helps us understand which channel an install came from).
  • Facebook SDK (Meta) — app-event analytics/attribution.
  • TikTok Business SDK — app-event analytics/attribution.

The analytics/attribution providers above may process your Android Advertising ID and standard app-event/diagnostic data (device model, OS version, app version, country/language, crash logs) under their own privacy policies. We do not share your photos, generated videos, or device identifier with these analytics/attribution providers.

You can reset or limit your Advertising ID at any time in your Android device's Settings ▸ Privacy ▸ Ads.

8. Notifications

The App requests permission to send notifications (Android 13+) so it can tell you when a video you started generating has finished, even if you've left the App. You can decline this permission and still use every other feature of the App normally; you can change your choice later in your device's App notification settings.

9. Your rights and "Delete my data" — please read this carefully

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your data, or to object to certain processing.

Settings ▸ Delete my data in the App deletes, on your device: your device identifier, your liked styles, your saved styles, your generation history, and cached files. After this, the App generates a new, unrelated device identifier if you continue using it.

Our AI processing provider (media-forge) does not currently offer an automated way for us to delete a photo you already uploaded or a video already generated on their server.
  • "Delete my data" does not remove photos you've already uploaded, or videos already generated, from our provider's server or its public CDN link. We want to be fully transparent about this limitation rather than promise a deletion we cannot yet perform automatically.
  • If you want a specific uploaded photo or generated video removed from our provider's infrastructure, contact us at cghotel1206@gmail.com with enough detail to identify it (for example, the approximate date/time and the style/template you used, or the result link if you still have it). We will forward every such request to our processing provider and ask them to remove it manually. Because this depends on a third party and is a manual process, we cannot guarantee a specific turnaround time or that removal will always be possible, but we will make a good-faith effort on every request we receive.

Contact us at cghotel1206@gmail.com for any other privacy request, including requests related to data protection law in your country.

We will update this section the moment our provider adds a self-service or automated deletion capability.
