1. No account, no sign-in

MotionCraft does not have user accounts, registration, or login (no email/password, no Google/Facebook sign-in). You can use every feature of the App immediately after install.

Instead of an account, the App generates a random device identifier (a UUID) the first time you open it, and stores it only on your device. This identifier is sent with each request to our server so it can show you your own liked styles, follows, comments, and creations, and is not linked to your name, email, phone number, or any other real-world identity we ask you for — because we never ask for one.

You can erase this identifier and everything tied to it at any time: Settings ▸ Delete my data. This permanently deletes, on our server, everything associated with your device identifier — profile, likes, follows, comments, generation tasks, results, and uploaded photos — and clears the same data stored locally on your device. This cannot be undone; after deletion the App creates a new, unrelated device identifier if you continue using it.

2. Photos you use in the App

MotionCraft lets you pick one or more photos from your device (using the Android system Photo Picker) to generate a stylized image or video from a template you choose.

  • We do not request access to your photo library. The system Photo Picker lets you hand us the specific photo(s) you choose without granting the App any broader permission.
  • Before a photo leaves your device, the App rotates it to the correct orientation and then strips all EXIF metadata, including GPS/location coordinates, resizes it, and compresses it. Your photo's location data never reaches our server.
  • The prepared photo is uploaded to our server solely to generate the result you asked for (the AI rendering you requested). Uploaded photos that are not used in a generation are automatically deleted from our server after 7 days. A photo attached to a generation task is kept for as long as that task/result exists, or until you delete it or delete your data.
  • Your uploaded photos are private to your device identifier — they are not shown in the public feed and are not used to train AI models beyond producing the result you requested.
  • Results you generate stay in the App ("My creations") until you delete them. You choose, per result, whether to save it to your device's photo gallery, share it outside the App, or post it to the in-app community — nothing is posted or saved automatically.

3. Community features

If you like a template, follow a creator, post a comment, or publish a creation to the community, that activity is sent to our server tied to your device identifier so other users can see counts, comments, and posts. Comments and posts are associated with a randomly generated display name (which you can change in your profile) — not with your real identity. We do not require or collect your real name, phone number, or email address for these features.

5. Third-party services

MotionCraft uses the following third-party services. Some are used for analytics and stability, and some are advertising/measurement SDKs bundled with the App's underlying platform even in builds where ads are not yet turned on (see the note below).

  • Firebase Analytics (Google) — app usage analytics.
  • Firebase Crashlytics (Google) — crash and stability reporting.
  • Firebase Remote Config (Google) — lets us adjust app behavior/configuration without a new release.
  • Google Mobile Ads / AdMob — the App's build includes Google's advertising SDK and a Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent component. In the current version of the App, ads are not served — this infrastructure exists so a future version can show ads, and where required by your region's privacy law (e.g., GDPR/UK, or a Google-defined US state), the App will present a consent/privacy choices prompt before any ad-related data is used. If and when ads are turned on in a later version, this Policy will be updated to describe the ad formats shown.
  • Adjust — mobile attribution/analytics (helps us understand which channel an install came from).
  • Facebook SDK (Meta) — app-event analytics/attribution.
  • TikTok Business SDK — app-event analytics/attribution.

These providers may process your Android Advertising ID and standard app-event/ diagnostic data (device model, OS version, app version, country/language, crash logs) under their own privacy policies. We do not share your photos, comments, or device identifier with these providers beyond what's needed for the analytics/attribution purposes above.

You can reset or limit your Advertising ID at any time in your Android device's Settings ▸ Privacy ▸ Ads.

6. Notifications

The App requests permission to send notifications (Android 13+) so it can tell you when a generation you started has finished, even if you've left the App. You can decline this permission and still use every other feature of the App normally; you can change your choice later in your device's App notification settings.

7. Data retention

  • Device-identifier-linked data (profile, likes, follows, comments, posts, generation tasks/results) is kept until you delete it via Settings ▸ Delete my data, or until you delete individual items in the App.
  • Uploaded photos not attached to a generation are deleted automatically after 7 days.
  • Diagnostic/analytics data collected by the third-party services in §5 is retained according to each provider's own policy.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your data, or to object to certain processing. For MotionCraft, the practical way to exercise these rights is:

  • Delete everything tied to your device identifier: Settings ▸ Delete my data (in-app), which removes your data from our server and your device immediately.
  • Contact us at cghotel1206@gmail.com for any other request, including requests related to data protection law in your country.

9. Children

MotionCraft is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required by your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us at cghotel1206@gmail.com and we will delete it.
